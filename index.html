Prior Elicitation Survey

Share your beliefs about a randomized field experiment before results are observed, following the Bayesian methodology of Iacovone, McKenzie & Meager (Econometrica, 2025).

The study. Eligible R&D-active German start-ups are randomized (N = 500, two arms). The treatment arm receives a LinkedIn message offering an AI-generated pre-filled Forschungszulage (R&D tax credit) draft application. Primary outcome: BSFZ application within 3 months. Baseline application rate ≈ 5%.
What you will do. Distribute 20 stones across bins for three questions. Each stone = 5% probability mass. Takes about 3–4 minutes.
